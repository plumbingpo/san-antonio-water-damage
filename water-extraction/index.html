Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. San Antonio Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout San Antonio using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Signs You Need Water Extraction in San Antonio

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Storm water collecting in the lowest level of the home
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

How fast and how completely water is extracted decides how much of your property is damaged and what restoration will cost. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
