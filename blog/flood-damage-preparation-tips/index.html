How to Prepare for Flooding in San Antonio, TX

Home / Blog / Flood Prevention for Homeowners in San Antonio, TX

While you can't control the weather, you can take proactive steps to protect your San Antonio, TX property from flood damage. Preparation significantly reduces the impact of flooding events and can save thousands in restoration costs. This guide from San Antonio Restoration Co covers the most effective preparation strategies for San Antonio homeowners.

Find Out How Flood-Prone You Are

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. Look up your address on the FEMA flood maps to see if it falls inside a designated flood zone. Being outside a high-risk zone does not make you safe, because most flood claims are filed for properties outside the designated flood zones. Consider drainage patterns, the lay of the land, and the distance to creeks or other waterways that heavy rain could push over their banks.

Protect Your Property

The main physical steps are to seal cracks in the foundation and apply a waterproof coating, grade the soil so it slopes away from the house, install a battery-backed sump pump and keep it maintained, clear the gutters and downspouts and carry their discharge 6 feet or more away from the foundation, raise HVAC equipment and electrical panels above likely flood levels, and store valuables and important documents above ground level.

Insurance Preparation

A typical homeowner's insurance policy excludes damage from rising floodwater. Flood policies are sold by the NFIP and by private carriers, and they usually come with a 30-day wait before coverage begins, so look into one long before any forecast forces the issue. Document your property and belongings with photos and video for insurance purposes. Store your policy paperwork where you can get to it, and learn your deductible and coverage limits.

Your Plan for a Flood Emergency

Planning has to happen before floodwater is on the way. Keep a packed go-bag ready, and be sure of your evacuation routes. Keep San Antonio Restoration Co's number ((938) 241-1772) in your phone for immediate post-flood professional cleanup. Keep contact details for your insurance agent somewhere easy to find. When a flood is in the forecast, carry valuables upstairs, shut off utilities if you are told to, and record the condition of your property before it arrives.
